# Immediate danger
If there is immediate danger, move to a safe location and contact local emergency services or the designated site emergency contact. Do not put yourself at risk to protect property.
# Reporting incidents
Report workplace injuries, near misses, unsafe conditions, and damaged safety equipment to the site safety contact as soon as practical. Include location, time, and a factual description.
# Evacuation
Follow posted evacuation routes and trained wardens' instructions. Do not re-enter until authorized. Keep emergency exits and access routes clear.
# Follow-up
The safety team records incidents, assesses corrective actions, and shares lessons while respecting privacy.
