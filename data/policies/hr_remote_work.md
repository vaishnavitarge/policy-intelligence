# Standard arrangement
Eligible employees may request up to 2 remote-work days per week, subject to manager approval, role suitability, customer commitments, and team coverage. A manager may approve a different arrangement in writing where business needs require it.
# Employee responsibilities
Employees working remotely must protect confidential information, use approved devices, follow information-security requirements, and remain reachable during agreed working hours.
# Exceptions
Long-term or cross-border arrangements must be reviewed by People Operations before they begin.
