# Phishing emails
Do not click links, open unexpected attachments, reply with credentials, or forward suspicious content externally. Report suspected phishing through the approved security-reporting channel. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, contact the security team immediately and follow its instructions.
# Authentication
Use multi-factor authentication where required. Never approve an authentication prompt you did not initiate. Do not share passwords, access tokens, or one-time codes.
# Devices and data
Use approved devices and software. Store business data only in approved locations. Report lost devices or suspected data exposure promptly.
# Reporting
Provide the incident time, affected device or account, and a short description. Do not attempt unauthorized investigation or delete evidence.
