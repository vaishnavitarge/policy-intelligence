# Purpose
This fictional policy demonstrates leave and carry-forward rules for a portfolio application.
# Carry-forward
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next leave year. Carry-forward requests must be recorded in the HR portal no later than 31 March. Days above the stated limit expire unless an authorized HR exception is documented.
# Requesting leave
Employees should submit planned leave in the HR portal and obtain manager approval before making non-refundable travel arrangements. Managers should review requests fairly, taking team coverage and business needs into account.
