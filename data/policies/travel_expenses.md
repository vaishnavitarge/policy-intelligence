# Expense submission deadline
Employees should submit business travel expense claims within 15 calendar days after the trip ends. Claims should include itemized receipts, business purpose, travel dates, and relevant project or cost centre.
# Meals
For this fictional example only, the standard meal reimbursement limit is INR 1,200 per traveller per day, subject to trip approval and receipts. Exceptions require written Finance approval before reimbursement.
# Approval
The employee's manager reviews business purpose and cost centre. Finance reviews receipt completeness and compliance. Missing receipts may delay processing.
# Questions
Contact Finance Operations for current approved limits. This fictional document must not be used to claim real expenses.
