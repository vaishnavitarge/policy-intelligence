# New equipment
Submit a laptop or accessory request through the IT service portal. Include business reason, manager, required-by date, and approved cost centre where applicable. The manager must approve before IT procurement begins.
# Repair and replacement
For a damaged or malfunctioning company device, raise a service ticket with the asset tag and issue description. Do not bypass security controls or use an unapproved repair provider.
# Return of equipment
Return company equipment when requested by IT or when employment or assignment ends. IT records the return and updates the asset register.
