# Eligibility
Privileged system access is restricted to authorized IT and system-administration personnel with an approved business need. General employees must not use another person's administrator account or attempt to elevate privileges.
# Request and approval
A privileged access request must identify the system, business justification, requested duration, and accountable manager. Approval from the system owner and Information Security is required before access is granted.
# Least privilege and review
Access must be limited to the minimum permissions and duration needed. Privileged access is logged and reviewed periodically. Emergency access follows a documented break-glass process and is reviewed after use.
# Revocation
Access should be removed promptly when the business need ends or the user's role changes.
