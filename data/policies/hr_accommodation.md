# Requesting an accommodation
An employee may contact People Operations to discuss a workplace accommodation. Describe the work-related barrier and adjustment requested using the confidential channel designated by People Operations.
# Review
People Operations coordinates a review with the employee and relevant parties. Information should be shared only with people who need it to evaluate or implement the request.
# Privacy
Avoid sending sensitive personal information to broad mailing lists. Contact People Operations for approved process and documentation requirements.
